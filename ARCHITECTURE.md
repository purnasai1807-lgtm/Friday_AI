# Friday AI Architecture

## Overview

Friday AI is organized as a modular platform for personal AI experiences spanning local intelligence, agent orchestration, automation, voice, vision, memory, retrieval, planning, plugins, tools, dashboards, APIs, analytics, security, and developer workflows.
